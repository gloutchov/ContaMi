01

Your starting point

A complete overview, without losing the details.

Net worth, liquidity, cash registers, properties, investments, pensions and recurring commitments stay visible and reconcilable.

02

Daily clarity

Follow every movement and every balance.

Searchable transactions, filters, accounts and cash registers reveal what happened, when it happened and how it affects liquidity.

03

Homes and income

Properties, utilities, taxes and rent in context.

Track valuations, costs, consumption, configurable taxes and rent payments with separate due periods and actual receipt dates.

04

Every kilometre counts

Understand the real cost of your vehicles.

Fuel, maintenance, insurance, taxes, tyres and optional financing combine into a clear cost history and vehicle comparison.

05

Long-term perspective

Capital invested and current value, side by side.

Contributions, liquidations, valuations and recurring plans remain connected to cash movements without inflating income or expenses.

06

A future without double counting

Private pensions and compartments stay distinct.

Collector funds, individual compartments, contributions and current values are aggregated once while preserving each position’s history.

07

Plan ahead

Recurring income, expenses and instalments that respect history.

Schedule future movements, confirm them safely and change future rates without rewriting operations already completed.

08

Clear balances together

Shared expenses without separate calculations.

Participants, personal shares, payer and reimbursement status remain linked to the original transaction and monthly totals.

09

Your setup, portable

Configure, import and keep control of the workbook.

Under the hood

Built to stay understandable, recoverable and yours.

Portable workbook
A readable .xlsx file is the canonical source. macOS can optionally keep a Numbers mirror.
Verified saves
Temporary write, re-read verification, recoverable backups and protection from external edits.
Hardened desktop app
Sandboxed Electron renderer, validated IPC, blocked navigation and no network during normal use.
Guided import
Versioned Excel templates, security preflight, row-level preview and atomic confirmation.
Bilingual and adaptive
Italian and English, system-aware light and dark themes, keyboard-friendly controls.
Cross-platform releases
Packages for Windows x64 and macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon, with SHA-256 checksums.
